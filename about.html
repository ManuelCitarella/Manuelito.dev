BIOGRAPHY

Emanuele Citarella
Professional full-stack developer
Bari, Italy

I am a professional full-stack developer based in Bari, Italy. Right now, I am especially interested in Linux and system administration: the quieter work of understanding what is happening underneath and keeping systems clear and reliable.

Outside work, I volunteer for my city, read, listen to music, write, and play games. I try to listen more than I speak, although I am still learning how to do that well. This site is one place where I collect the ideas, projects, and small things that stay with me.
